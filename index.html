Green Frog Stats

  • Size: 5cm to 11 cm
  • Weight: 28g to 85g
  • Lifespan: ~ 6 years in the wild
  • Conservation Status: Least Concern

About the Green Frog

The green frog (Lithobates Clamitans or Rana Clamitans) is one of the species of frogs native to eastern North America. They have two subspecies: the bronze frog and the northern green frog. They typically have green heads and noticable tympanums (the circle near the eye) on their sides. On the other hand, their body is brown, grey, or dark green. These frogs are very territorial. (Lithobates clamitans, 2026)
